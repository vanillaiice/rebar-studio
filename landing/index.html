Your data stays with you

Studio stores templates and documents on your device. It has no account, no analytics and no upload: it only loads its own files. Back up from Settings to keep a copy.

In beta

What it does not do yet

It does not import Word, Excel or PDF files: templates are written in .reb source. No sync between devices. No macOS installer (use the web app).

FAQ

Common questions

Read the user guide
Is it really free?+

Yes. Studio is free and open source (GPL-3.0), with no account and no subscription. Rebuilding a form for you is a separate service; we tell you the cost before any work.

Can it open my Word or Excel form?+

No. The form is rebuilt once as a .reb template. You can do it yourself from a sample, or send us a blank copy and we will.

Do I need to know how to code?+

Filling documents does not. Creating or editing a template uses .reb source, which combines HTML with form fields. Samples and editor snippets are included.

Does anything leave my computer?+

No. Studio keeps templates and documents on your device and sends nothing to us.

What is the difference between web and desktop?+

The desktop app exports PDFs directly. The web app uses the browser's print dialog. Mixed portrait and landscape pages need the desktop app.

How do I back up or move my work?+

In Settings, choose "Back up now" to export a .rebbackup file and restore it on another device. Single documents move as .rebdoc files.
